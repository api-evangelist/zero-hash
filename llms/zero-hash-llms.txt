# zerohash - The Operating System for Digital Money
> Crypto, stablecoin, and tokenization infrastructure for banks, brokerages, and fintechs
zerohash is a regulated infrastructure provider that enables seamless integration of crypto, stablecoins, and tokenized assets. Trusted by companies like Stripe, Interactive Brokers, Morgan Stanley, and Gusto to power on-chain services for millions of customers. $65B+ in transaction volume settled across 200+ jurisdictions with 99.9% uptime.
